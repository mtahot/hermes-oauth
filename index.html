HermesAgent

Personal automation assistant — single-user, non-commercial.

HermesAgent is a private, self-hosted software agent operated by a single individual for their own personal productivity. It connects to that person's own Google account to read and send mail, manage calendar events, files, tasks and contacts on their behalf.

How it works

  • The application runs on a private server controlled by its owner.
  • Google authorisation is granted by the account owner through standard OAuth 2.0; no credentials are collected from anyone else.
  • No account is created, no data is sold, no advertising is involved.
